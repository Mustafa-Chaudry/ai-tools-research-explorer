Topics I'm Exploring

Artificial intelligence is developing quickly, particularly in areas where AI systems are moving beyond generating text and starting to use tools, make decisions and complete tasks.

These are some of the areas I'm interested in:

  • Agentic AI - Autonomous agents, tool use and multi-agent systems.
  • Digital Transformation - Transforming businesses and society through digital technologies.
  • Large Language Models - LLM capabilities, applications and limitations.
  • AI Governance - Responsible AI, transparency and risk management.
  • Healthcare AI - Applications of AI in healthcare and public services.

The Idea

Finding useful AI tools and the research behind them often means searching different websites separately. I want to make that process easier.

The goal is to bring projects and publications together so users can search by topic, discover relevant resources and explore them from one place.

Future Development: This website will eventually allow users to search for AI topics and find both open-source tools and research papers more efficiently.
