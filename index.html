Topics I'm Exploring

Artificial intelligence is developing quickly, particularly in areas where AI systems are moving beyond generating text and starting to use tools, make decisions and complete tasks.

These are some of the areas I'm interested in:

  • Agentic AI - Autonomous agents, tool use and multi-agent systems.
  • Digital Transformation - Transforming businesses and society through digital technologies.
  • Large Language Models - LLM capabilities, applications and limitations.
  • AI Governance - Responsible AI, transparency and risk management.
  • Healthcare AI - Applications of AI in healthcare and public services.
  • AI Engineering & Systems Architecture - Designing, integrating and scaling reliable AI-powered systems.

Understanding the results

GitHub repositories

GitHub results show open-source projects, including their descriptions, programming languages and star counts. The More details button can also show a repository's licence, open issues and last update date.

Research publications

Crossref provides publication records, including titles, authors, dates, source information and DOI links when available.

Search limitations

The application retrieves six results from each selected source. The relevance order comes from the providers. The alphabetical order applies only to the results already retrieved.

The Idea

Finding useful AI tools and the research behind them often means searching different websites separately. I wanted to make that process easier.

The aim is to bring projects and publications together so users can search by topic, find relevant resources and explore them from one place. This application is a prototype that I have developed for my Web Technologies Module as part of my Msc Computer Science with Artifical Intelligence degree and provides a starting point for further development.
